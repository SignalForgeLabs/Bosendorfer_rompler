Welcome to the Classic Vienna 290 Installer

This installer will guide you through installing the Signal Forge Audio Labs Classic Vienna 290 97-key concert grand piano on your Mac.

Features Included:

  • Universal Binary Architecture: Native execution on both Apple Silicon (arm64) and Intel (x86_64) Macs.
  • Plugin Formats: VST3, AudioUnit (AU), and Standalone application.
  • Acoustic Split & Studio Mics: 4 selectable microphones (Neumann KM 184, Neuman TLM 103, Neumann U87, RCA 44BX NEVE 1073) with per-voice Bass/Treble crossover, physical damper mechanics, and 6 selectable concert hall & venue impulse responses.

Default Installation Paths:

  • VST3: /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Classic Vienna 290.vst3
  • AU: /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Classic Vienna 290.component
  • Standalone: /Applications/Classic Vienna 290.app
