1. OVERVIEW
-----------
The SignalForgeLabs Bosendorfer Imperial 290 is a 97-key concert grand piano
virtual instrument featuring dual embedded SoundFont banks (Standard & Brite),
per-voice equal-power Bass/Treble microphone split convolution with 4 studio
microphones (Neumann KM 184, Neuman TLM 103, Neumann U87, RCA 44BX NEVE 1073),
physical sustain pedal damper mechanics (AuthenticDamperClunk), and 3 concert
hall impulse responses.

2. INSTALLATION PATHS
---------------------

WINDOWS:
- VST3 Plugin: C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\Bosendorfer Imperial 290.vst3
- Standalone App: C:\Program Files\SignalForgeLabs\Bosendorfer Imperial 290\Bosendorfer Imperial 290.exe

MACOS:
- VST3 Plugin: /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Bosendorfer Imperial 290.vst3
- AudioUnit (AU): /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Bosendorfer Imperial 290.component
- Standalone App: /Applications/Bosendorfer Imperial 290.app

LINUX:
- VST3 Plugin: ~/.vst3/Bosendorfer Imperial 290.vst3
- Standalone App: ~/.local/bin/bosendorfer-imperial-290
