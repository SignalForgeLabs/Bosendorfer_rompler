1. OVERVIEW
-----------
The Signal Forge Audio Labs Classic Vienna 290 is a 97-key concert grand piano
virtual instrument featuring dual embedded SoundFont banks (Vienna_290_Full.sf2
& Vienna_290_Brite.sf2), per-voice equal-power Bass/Treble microphone split
convolution with 4 studio microphones (Neumann KM 184, Neuman TLM 103,
Neumann U87, RCA 44BX NEVE 1073), physical sustain pedal damper mechanics,
and 6 selectable concert hall & venue impulse responses.

2. INSTALLATION PATHS
---------------------

WINDOWS:
- VST3 Plugin: C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\Classic Vienna 290.vst3
- Standalone App: C:\Program Files\Signal Forge Audio Labs\Classic Vienna 290\Classic Vienna 290.exe

MACOS:
- VST3 Plugin: /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Classic Vienna 290.vst3
- AudioUnit (AU): /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Classic Vienna 290.component
- Standalone App: /Applications/Classic Vienna 290.app

LINUX:
- VST3 Plugin: ~/.vst3/Classic Vienna 290.vst3
- Standalone App: ~/.local/bin/classic-vienna-290
